# Fonts

`kfgqpc_hafs_v30.ttf`, `kfgqpc_warsh_v30.ttf` and `kfgqpc_qalun_v30.ttf` are the King Fahd Glorious
Quran Printing Complex's mushaf fonts, version 3.0, from its developer packages
(https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/quran-dev/), unchanged. The Complex allows their free use, copying and
distribution, as long as they are not sold and not modified.
