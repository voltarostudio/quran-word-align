- **The recordings and their ayah timings** are from [mp3quran.net](https://www.mp3quran.net). The
  recordings belong to their reciters. mp3quran.net states that any visitor or developer may use any
  material or link from the site («جميع الحقوق متاحة للجميع ويحق لأي زائر أو مطور استخدام اي مادة أو
  رابط من الموقع»). The dataset does not include any recording; it points to mp3quran.net's files.
  Credit them: «التلاوات وتوقيت الآيات من موقع mp3quran.net».
- **The Quran text** (`hafs-words.json`) is the Hafs text of the
  [King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex](https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/quran-dev/), used unchanged.
  The Complex allows free use, copying and distribution, commercial included, as long as the mushaf is
  not sold and the files are not modified. Credit: «نص المصحف من مجمع الملك فهد لطباعة المصحف الشريف».
- **The model** the timings were made with, [quran-asr-tiny](https://huggingface.co/Abdou/quran-asr-tiny),
  is by Abdou, under the MIT licence.
